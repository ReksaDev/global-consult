Privacy Notice

How information submitted through this website is handled.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Information collected

If you submit an enquiry, the website collects information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, organisation, area of interest and message.

Why it is used

Information is used to review and respond to your enquiry, assess whether consulting support may be suitable, and maintain a record of business communications.

Storage and access

Enquiry submissions are stored using the website platform's lead-capture service and are accessible to the site owner for follow-up. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers or sensitive personal information.

Sharing

Information is not intentionally sold. It may be processed by website hosting and form-service providers as needed to operate the website and handle enquiries.

Retention and requests

Enquiry information is retained only as long as reasonably needed for business follow-up and recordkeeping. To request access, correction or deletion, contact the site owner through the same channel used to submit your enquiry and identify the relevant submission.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the service develops. The latest version will be published on this page.

This is an initial privacy notice for a basic enquiry form. It should be reviewed for the business's actual legal entity, jurisdiction and data practices before broader commercial launch.
